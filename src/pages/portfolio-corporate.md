## Solway Primary School ID

The brief was to update the existing Solway Primary School logo, to create a definitive logo and apply the designed style to all their collateral and signage, thus eliminating the many different versions of logos they had been using.

## Village Green ID

With a management changed-over, Village Green needed a fresh new look to differentiate it from its previous persona. We drew inspiration from their different disciplines about the environment and then brought them together to create to a logo of sustainability.
