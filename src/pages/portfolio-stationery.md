## Kylie Sturgess business cards

Kylie Sturgess is a Perth based writer, researcher, interviewer, podcaster, nationally and internationally published author and her tango is something to be seen. Kylie needed a business card to match her infectious enthusiam for science, skepticism and critical thinking, so a slick double sided matt laminate business card with a spot vanish on the front, and her beloved Pod Black Cat drawing on the back was created. Purrrfect!
