## Baby Wasp

Baby Wasp is the brain child of a young and energetic entrepreneur, Courtney Beck. She has always had the nickname of Wasp, so her choice of Baby Wasp for her label was an obvious one - she wanted to launch into the fashion industry like a little social insect with a sting in her tail. Perfect! Courtney has been thrilled with the cute and cheeky Baby Wasp logo that Faster Pussycat Productions has produced.
