## The Chaser’s _Age of Terror_ Variety Hour

The Chaser adopted a purely user-pays approach by mounting a national tour of a new live production from March to July 2008\. With a massive advertising blitz, Faster Pussycat Productions pounced on board, sending off press ads with catlike reflexes to Canberra, Parramatta, Townsville, Cairns, Perth, Darwin, Ballarat, Hobart, Sydney, Adelaide, Geelong, Melbourne, Brisbane and Newcastle.
