## A tiny sample

I have been creating graphics for tv and film for over 12 years, working with a multitude of creative people from designers, art directors, props managers and production designers. The graphics they have required have been anything from newspapers, magazines, signage, photographs, packaging to websites on laptops, desktop computers and iPads. You name it and I think I may have already created it once or twice before. Here is a tiny sample of graphics I created for the Kath and Kim telemovie: Da Kath And Kim Code.
